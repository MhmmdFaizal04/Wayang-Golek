Wayang Kulit

Two puppets,
one in each hand

Play two leather shadow puppets on the lamp-lit kelir. Your webcam tracks both hands, just as a dalang holds the sticks.

  • Left & right handEach hand holds one character: the left hand plays the left puppet, the right hand the right one.
  • PalmThe body stick (gapit). The puppet follows it.
  • Thumb & indexThat puppet's two arm rods (tuding). Spread, raise or pinch them.
  • Tilt handThe puppet leans with you.
  • Closer to cameraLifts that puppet off the screen, so its shadow grows and softens.
  • Little fingerShow only your pinky: that puppet performs its kiprahan, a signature dance on the gamelan beat.
